# Phase 1 staging contract

- Levels 1–2: direct addition
- Levels 3–4: direct subtraction
- Levels 5–8: small-friend operations
- Levels 9–12: big-friend operations
- Levels 13–15: mixed mastery
- Exact answer equality only
- 3 consecutive correct answers advance one level
- 2 consecutive wrong answers lower one level, with Level 1 as the floor
- Consecutive-wrong state survives result-screen navigation and refresh
- Rule-specific correct/wrong counters persist locally
- No impossible negative subtraction or invalid friend-rule fallback
- Level 15 cannot advance beyond 15

This contract is staging-only until verified on device and merged to production.
