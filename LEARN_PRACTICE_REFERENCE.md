# Learn + Practice Reference Notes

Reviewed 2026-09-16 for product design only.

## External references
- Abacus Kids - Mental Math (Google Play / App Store): Level 1 uses 5 beginner lessons with Learn, Practice and Quiz phases; Level 2 introduces direct operations before Small Friend/Big Friend formulas and explicitly explains why/when formulas are used; practice sets are generated fresh; daily streak and saved results are visible.
- KidsUP Soroban: guided lessons, tutor-led instruction, game-based drills, and parent progress.
- Sorokid: adventure-map presentation, mascot-led learning, daily quests and rewards.
- Practice Abacus: touch-first abacus, progressive curriculum, progress dashboards, instant feedback/confetti, speed drills.
- Vedaavi Abacus Child Learning App: step-by-step and final-answer practice modes, visual bead cues, guided learning, exams and progress tracking.

## What we take
- Structured phases.
- Demonstrate before drill.
- Explain the reason behind a movement/formula.
- Visual bead cues.
- Short repeatable sessions.
- Fresh problem variation.
- Strong progress/reward loop.
- Adventure/mascot presentation.

## What we do differently
Babi is our own character and visual identity. We keep the abacus itself as the learning hero and use cartoons to explain, celebrate and guide rather than clutter the calculation area.

No competitor assets, names, branding, text, or distinctive artwork are copied into the product.
